Worksheet Answers

Q1: Award one mark each for correctly labelling the wheel and axle.

Q2: 12 cm.

Q3: 4 cm = 30 N, 8 cm = 15 N, 12 cm = 10 N.

Q4: Example: As wheel radius increased from 4 cm to 12 cm, the force needed decreased from 30 N to 10 N.

Q5: A larger wheel lets a smaller effort turn the axle, but the hand would move through a greater distance.

Q6: Mechanical advantage values are 2, 4 and 6.

Source Notes

The science basis is the lesson content: a wheel and axle are attached, the axle turns with the wheel, and effort applied to a larger wheel can reduce the force needed to turn the axle. [S01]

The worksheet uses the source question style: labelling, prediction, data recording, pattern description, explanation and later calculation. [S02]
